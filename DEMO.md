# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Record one screen. Use two wallets (two browsers or a normal and a private window). Both game wallets need STT for gas and about 60 USDso (stake plus bankroll). Keep keys, PINs and contract addresses off screen.

**Before recording:** deploy, build, create both wallets, fund them, and check that a match opens. Set a short lobby if you can.

1. **The idea (15 s).** "Bulls against Bears on real dreamDEX perps. You are drafted at random, you trade from your own on-chain vault, and the team with the better average gain wins."
2. **The 3D world (15 s).** Walk to a market door, show the match wall and the live chart in the market room.
3. **Open a room (20 s).** Press "Open a new match", type a room size (try 25 to show the error, then 4), confirm the seat. Show the progress popup, then the arena: your bull or bear is seated and the board shows your team.
4. **Second player (20 s).** In the other window take a seat in the same room. Show the team drawn at the seat (opposite the first), and both models sitting on their stools.
5. **Match starts (15 s).** Lobby locks, characters stand and pull the rope. Show the score bar.
6. **Trade (40 s).** From each wallet press Buy or Sell with a position size. Show the vault position and the real transaction. Optionally start the bot box with the example script on one wallet.
7. **Result (30 s).** When time is up the match settles by itself (or press Settle). Show the winning team on the board and the score bar, then Claim: payout plus bankroll back.
8. **Trust (20 s).** Say what is on chain (scores, vaults, payouts), who can do what (operator only collects the rake), and the honest limits from `SECURITY.md`.

**If something fails on camera:** read the on-screen error, it is written in plain words, and show it. Do not hide failures: say what they mean.
